Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

What Pulse does

Pulse measures your internet speed by exchanging test data with Cloudflare's edge network. Your recent test results are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device. You can clear them any time with the Clear button.

What we collect

We run no accounts and no analytics on this site, and we do not sell personal data. Speed tests contact Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com), which can see your IP address to route the test, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Advertising and affiliate links

Pulse may show ads served by Google AdSense and may include affiliate links. If you buy through an affiliate link, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Ad partners may use cookies to show ads based on your browsing; you can opt out of personalized ads in your Google account settings or at aboutads.info.

Cookies

Pulse itself sets no tracking cookies. Third-party ad services, when enabled, may set their own cookies as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
