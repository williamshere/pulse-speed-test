Pulse measures real throughput using parallel connections to Cloudflare's edge network (speed.cloudflare.com). Results depend on Wi-Fi, device, and network load. For comparison, 25 Mbps handles one 4K stream, 10 Mbps a smooth HD video call, and under 30 ms ping feels responsive for gaming.

How Pulse works

  1. Ping — sends tiny requests to the edge and times the round trip. Lower is better. Jitter is how much that time varies.
  2. Download — opens several parallel downloads and counts bytes arriving over time to calculate Mbps.
  3. Upload — posts generated data back to the edge and measures how fast it leaves your device.

Nothing is uploaded or stored on a server. History lives only in your browser's local storage.
